### 重点难点深度剖析

> [!warning] Q6 · 倒装句型（so + 助动词 + 主语）
> - **考点规律**：表示“某人/某物也一样”用 `so + be动词/助动词/情态动词 + 主语`；助动词的时态必须与前句保持一致。
> - **错因剖析**：前句为 `our life styles are changing`（现在进行时 are），后句主语是复数 `our bodies`，因此倒装结构为 `so are our bodies`。
> - **关联知识库**：[[倒装 inversion]] · [[主谓一致 subject verb agreement]]

> [!warning] Q10 · 并列谓语 vs. 分词伴随状语
> - **考点规律**：英语单句中两个谓语动词之间必须有连词（如 and, but）；若没有连词，次要动作必须退化为**非谓语动词**（分词或不定式）。
> - **错因剖析**：`seized the girl and took her away` 由 and 连接两个并列谓语；其后的 `, disappearing into the woods` 没有连词，必须用现在分词作伴随状语。
> - **关联知识库**：[[动词分类 verb Classification]] · [[非谓语动词 non-finite verb]]

> [!warning] Q11 · It struck sb. that... vs. It occurred to sb. that...
> - **考点规律**：
>   - `It struck sb. (that / how...)` —— **及物动词**，直接加人称宾格（如 struck me）。
>   - `It occurred to sb. (that / how...)` —— **不及物动词**，必须带介词 `to`。
> - **错因剖析**：题干为 `It suddenly ________ me how...`，空格后直接是 `me`，无介词 `to`，因此只能选 `struck`（D 项）。
> - **关联知识库**：[[动词分类 verb Classification]] · [[Word Contrasts]]

> [!warning] Q12 · 定语从句关系代词与及物动词 visit
> - **考点规律**：先行词虽然是地点（the place），但如果定语从句中的动词是**及物动词**且缺少宾语（`visit the place`），必须使用关系代词 `that` 或 `which`；只有当从句不缺主宾表（即先行词作状语）时才用关系副词 `where`。
> - **错因剖析**：误把地点名词一律等同于 where，忽视了 visit 的及物性。正确结构为 `the place that I want to visit most`。
> - **关联知识库**：[[从句 clause]]

> [!warning] Q14 · as if 引导方式状语从句的虚拟语气
> - **考点规律**：`as if / as though`（仿佛，好像）引导的从句若与当前事实相反，谓语动词使用一般过去时；若为 be 动词，正式语体中不论主语人称一律用 `were`。
> - **错因剖析**：该年轻男子并非日本人，与现在事实相反，故用 `as if he were a Japanese`。
> - **关联知识库**：[[虚拟语气 Subjunctive Mood]]

### 原文与参考译文

- **考试原文**：
  > The traditional Chinese hospitality requires food diversity, so that guests will be full before eating up all the dishes. A typical Chinese banquet menu includes cold dishes served at the beginning, followed by hot dishes, such as meat, poultry, vegetables, etc. At most banquets, the whole fish is considered to be essential, unless various kinds of seafood have been served already. Today, Chinese people would like to combine Western specialties with traditional Chinese dishes. Therefore, it is not rare to see steak being served as well. Salad is gaining popularity, although traditionally the Chinese people generally do not eat any food without cooking. There is usually at least a bowl of soup, served at the beginning or in the end of the dinner party. Desserts and fruit usually mark the end of the feast.

- **参考译文**：
  > 中国传统的待客之道讲究菜肴丰盛多样，以便客人在吃光所有菜之前就能吃饱。一份典型的中式宴席菜单包括开席时上的凉菜，随后是肉类、禽类、蔬菜等热菜。在大多数宴席上，除非已经上了各色海鲜，否则整条鱼被认为是必不可少的。如今，中国人喜欢将西方特色菜与中国传统菜肴相结合。因此，宴席上看到牛排也屡见不鲜。尽管传统上中国人一般不吃任何未经烹饪的生食，但沙拉现在也越来越受欢迎。通常至少会有一道汤，在宴会开始或结束时奉上。甜点和水果通常标志着宴席的结束。

### 核心词汇与考点速记

- `hospitality` [n.] 好客、殷勤、待客之道
- `food diversity` 菜肴多样性、菜品丰盛
- `poultry` [n.] 家禽、禽肉
- `essential` [adj.] 必不可少的、核心的
- `combine A with B` 将 A 与 B 结合
- `Western specialties` 西式特色菜肴
- `It is not rare to see...` 看到……并不罕见 / 屡见不鲜（双重否定强调）
- `gain popularity` 越来越受欢迎、流行开来
- `mark the end of the feast` 标志着盛宴的结束

It is well known that electricity is very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, electricity is helpful to us in many ways. We use electricity for lighting, cooking, studying and working. Without electricity, many machines cannot work properly.

However, we should save electricity in our daily life. First of all, we should turn off the lights when we leave a room. In addition, we should turn of computers and televisions when thy are not in use. Last but not least, we can use energy-saving appliances in our daily life.

As far as I am concerned, electricity is important, helpful and absolutely necessity.
