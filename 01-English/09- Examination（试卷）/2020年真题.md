## Part IV Translation（英译汉·单题）

> [!note] 📊 模块得分：` 9 / 15 分 `

随着中国的改革开放，许多年轻人人倾向于西式婚礼。新娘在婚礼上穿着白色婚纱，因为白色是一个纯洁的象征。
然而，中国的传统文化中白色往往用于葬礼，所以一定要记住白色的花一定不能作为礼物送给病人，尤其是老人或病危的病人。
同样，礼金不能装在白色的信封里，而应装在红色信封里。

- cash gift：礼金 
- in critical condition：病危；处于危急状态

## Part V Writing（短文写作·单题）

> [!note] 📊 模块得分：` / 15 分 `

It is well known that tour is very popular and trendy in our life. As a matter of fact, traveling is good for us in many ways. Some people enjoy visiting cities, while others like climbing mountains. As for me, I enjoy climbing Wutong Mountain. Here are the reasons.

First of all, It is obvious that climbing mountains can help us stay healthy. In addition, There is no doubt that climbing mountains make us happy and we can have fun with our friends. Last but not least, It goes without saying that climbing mountains can save money for us.

As far as I am concerned, traveling and climbing mountains are very important, helpful and absolutely necessary.
