# 题目

Letter of Invitation

假如你是李华，请写一封邮件邀请你的外国朋友 Tom 参加学校举办的英语角活动。内容包括：
1. 活动时间和地点；
2. 活动内容；
3. 邀请他参加。

# 范文骨架

## 邀请信留空临摹练习

> 练习要求：先看中文提示，在横线处默写英文。写完后再对照下面的参考答案。

---

### 第一段：点题——写信目的

```text
Dear Tom,

I am writing to [invite you to join us for the English Corner].
（我写信是想邀请你参加我们的英语角活动。）

I think it would be a good idea if you participated in [the English Corner].
（我认为如果你参加英语角会是个好主意。）

The [activity] will be held [this Friday evening].
（活动将于本周五晚上举行。）

```

---

### 第二段：写活动细节

```text
The activity will be held [at 7:00 p.m. in the school library].
（活动将于晚上七点在学校图书馆举行。）

Many [students and teachers will take part in this activity].
（许多学生和老师会参加。）

First of all, we will [discuss English study].
（首先，我们将讨论英语学习。）

In addition, we will [share some interesting stories].
（此外，我们将分享一些有趣的故事。）

Last but not least, we will [play language games together].
（最后同样重要的是，我们将一起玩语言游戏。）
```

---

### 第三段：总结——表达期待

```text
I am sure that [you will have a good time there].
（我相信你会在那里玩得很开心。）

I sincerely hope that [you can make it].
（我真诚地希望你能来。）

I am looking forward to [your reply].
（我期待你的回复。）

Yours,
Li Hua
```

---

### 替换练习：生日聚会

**题目：** 假如你是李华，邀请你的朋友 Tom 参加你的生日聚会。聚会于本周六下午 6 点在你家举行；活动包括吃晚餐、听音乐、玩游戏。

```text
Dear Tom,

I am writing to invite you to join us for [my birthday party]. I think it would be a good idea if you participate in it.

The activity will be held [at 6:00 p.m. this Saturday] at my home. I am sure that your presence will add more charm to [the party]. Many of my friends will join us, and I am sure that you will have an unforgettable time there.

Please let me know whether you can come. My phone number is [13888888888]. I sincerely hope that you can make it. I am looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Li Hua
```
