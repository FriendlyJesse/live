# 题目

My Hobby

写作要求：
- 你的爱好是什么？
- 为什么你有这个爱好？

# 范文骨架

## 练习

### 第一段：点题

It is well known that hobbies are very important in our lives.
As a matter of fact, hobbies are good for us in many ways.
As for me, my hobby is **reading**.
Here are the reasons.

### 第二段：写三个理由

First of all, reading can ==enrich my knowledge==.

In addition, reading can ==help me relax after a busy day==.

Last but not least, reading can ==help me develop a good habit and make my life more meaningful==.

### 第三段：总结

As far as I am concerned, reading is
==important, helpful, and absolutely necessary== .

## Reading Is My Hobby

```text
It is well known that hobbies are very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, music is good for us in many ways. Some people enjoy dancing, while others like reading. As for me, my hobby is reading. Here are the reasons.

First of all, it is obvious that reading can enrich my knowledge and broaden my horizons. In addition, there is no doubt that reading can help me relax after a busy day. Last but not least, it goes without saying that reading can help me develop a good reading habit and make my life more meaningful.

As far as I am concerned, reading is important, helpful and absolutely necessary.
```
