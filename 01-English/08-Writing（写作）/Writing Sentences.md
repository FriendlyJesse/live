# 写作句式

## 第一步：一句话的骨架

先认识 4 个最重要的成分：
- **主语（S）**：谁、什么。I, we, my parents, English, reading books。
- **谓语/动词（V）**：做什么、是什么、怎么样。study, like, is, are, helps。
- **宾语（O）**：动作作用在谁或什么上。English, books, my parents。
- **补语（C）**：说明主语或宾语的身份、状态、特征。happy, useful, a student。

最基本的英语句子不是“中文逐词翻译”，而是先找骨架：`谁/什么 + 做什么/是什么/怎么样`。

例如：
- I study English.
- English is important.
- Exercise makes me healthy.

把下面 5 个句型练熟，你就有了写作文最核心的工具。

### 主语 + 动词（S + V）

>表示“谁做了什么”，但动作后面不一定需要宾语。

- I study every day.
- My parents work hard.
- We exercise after school.
- English helps.

### 主语 + 动词 + 宾语 （S + V + O）

- I like English.
- I read books.
- We need exercise.
- Students use the Internet.
- My teacher encourages me.

写作时可以先准备动词：

| 想表达的意思 | 常用动词              | 示例                            |
| ------ | ----------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 喜欢     | like, love, enjoy | I enjoy reading.              |
| 学习     | learn, study      | I study English every day.    |
| 做      | do, make          | I do some exercise.           |
| 帮助     | help              | Reading helps me relax.       |
| 提高     | improve           | Practice improves my writing. |
| 需要     | need              | We need enough sleep.         |
| 认为     | think, believe    | I think it is useful.         |

### 主语 + be + 表语（S + be + C）

>be 动词包括：**am / is / are / was / were**。它不表示具体动作，而是表示“是”“处于某种状态”。

- I am a student.
- English is important.
- Books are useful.
- My school life is busy.
- The exercise is good for my health.

这是议论文、观点类作文的“主力句型”，因为你经常需要评价事物：
- It is useful.
- It is necessary.
- It is important.
- It is good for us.
- It is a good habit.

### 主语 + 动词 + 间接宾语 + 直接宾语（S + V + 人 + 物）

>表示“给某人某物、告诉某人某事、教某人某内容”。

- My teacher teachers me English.
- My parents give me support.
- Reading gives us knowledge.
- The book tells us a story.

这类句子很适合写“某事的好处”。
- Exercise gives us energy.
- Travelling gives us new experiences.
- Reading gives me knowledge and pleasure.

### 主语 + 动词 + 并与 + 宾语补足语（S + V + O +C）

>表示“使某人/某物怎么样”。

- Exercise makes me healthy.
- English makes my life better.
- Music makes me happy.
- The Internet makes learning easier.

>这个句型非常实用。你只要记住：
>A makes B + 形容词 / 名词

如：
- Reading makes me relaxed.
- Doing sports makes us stronger.
- Hard work makes people successful.

## 第二步：给短句加信息

>你的作文写不长，通常不是因为“没有高级词”，而是因为句子只写了骨架，没有补充信息。

看同一个意思怎样一步步变长：
1. I study.
2. I study English.
3. I study English every day.
4. I study English for one hour every day.
5. I study English for one hour every day because I want to pass the exam.

### 你可以从 4 个方向扩句

| 信息    | 常见问题      | 常用表达                                                   |
| ----- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 时间    | 什么时候？多久？  | every day, every morning, in my free time, after class |
| 地点    | 在哪里？      | at home, at school, in the library                     |
| 方式    | 怎样做？      | carefully, actively, with my friends                   |
| 原因/目的 | 为什么？为了什么？ | because..., to..., so that...                          |
### 最实用的扩句公式

- S + V + O + 时间
	- I read English articles every morning.
- S + V + O + 地点
	- I study English in the library.
- S + V + O + 方式
	- I listen to English carefully.
- S + V + O + because + 原因
	- I like reading because it is relaxing.
- To + 动词原形, S + V + O
	- To important my English, I read English articles every day.
## 第三步：把句子连起来

作文不是很多句子随意排在一起，而是句子之间要有逻辑。简单句、并列句和复合句搭配使用，文章会更自然；并列句可用 `and、but、or、so` 连接，而 `because、when、if、although` 等可引出从属信息。

| 逻辑  | 连接词                    | 例句                                          |
| --- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 补充  | and, also, besides     | I read books, and I also take notes.        |
| 转折  | but, however           | I am busy, but I study every day.           |
| 原因  | because                | I like English because it is useful.        |
| 结果  | so                     | English is important, so I study it hard.   |
| 举例  | for example            | For example, I read short English articles. |
| 顺序  | first, second, finally | First, I make a plan.                       |


### 并列句：句子 + and/but/so + 句子

>注意：and / but / so 前面通常加逗号；后面必须又是一个完整句子。

1. I am busy, but I study English every day.
2. Reading is interesting, and it gives make knowledge.
3. English is useful, so I want to learn it well.

### 原因句：句子 + because + 句子

1. I like reading because it makes me relaxed.
2. I exercise every week because I want to stay healthy.
3. I study English hard because it is important for my future.


## 第四步：一个段落怎么写

一个合格段落不是“想到什么写什么”，而是：

>主题句 + 原因/解释 + 例子 + 小结

以题目 **“Why is reading important?”（为什么阅读很重要？）** 为例：

Reading is very important to me.
First, it gives me a lot of knowledge.
For example, I can learn about history, science and different cultures from books.
Second, reading helps me relax after a busy day.
Therefore, I try to read for a least thirty minutes every day.

这段只有 55 词左右，但结构已经完整：
- 第 1 句：观点
- 第 2 句：第一个理由
- 第 3 句：例子
- 第 4 句：第二个理由
- 第 5 句：总结/行动

你会发现，它没有故意用罕见单词，也没有极难语法，但已经是一段“像作文”的英语。
## 第五步：稳定写到 100 词

>最容易的方法不是憋一大段，而是写 **3 段 × 每段 35–45 词**。

### 第一段：开头，约 25–35 词

- 介绍话题
- 直接说观点

重要：
>Nowadays, __ is becoming more and more important in our daily life.
>As a matter of fact, __ are good for us in many ways.
>As for me, __ .
>There are the reasons.

流行：
>Nowadays, __ is becoming more and more popular and trendy in our daily life.
>As a matter of fact, __ are good for us in many ways.
>In my opinion,  __ are good for us in many ways.
>There are the reasons.

### 第二段：主体，约 45–55 词

- 写两个理由
- 至少有一个例子
- 用 `First of all, In addition, Lat but not least` 连接

>First of all, It is obvious that __ can help us.
>This is because __ .
>For example, __ .
>In addition, there is no doubt that __ .
>Last but not least, It goes no saying that __ .

### 第三段：结尾，约 25–35 词

- 重申观点
- 写建议、计划或期待

>As far as I am concerned __ is very important, helpful and absolutely necessary.

### 利弊型

AI 是帮手还是对手

```text
  Nowadays, __(主题) is becoming more and more important in our daily lives. As a matter of fact, __(主题) is useful to us in many ways. Some people worry that it will __(现象一) , while others believe that __(现象二) can improve our lives. In my opinion, __(主题) has both advantages and disadvantages. It depends on how we use it.

  On the one hand, __(主题) helps us __(好处) . For example, __(好处的例子). On the other hand, if we __(坏处) , we may __(坏处的结果) .

  As far as I am concerned, __(主题) can make our lives better. We should use it wisely and keep __(好的行为).
```

```text
  Nowadays, artificial intelligence is becoming more and more important in our daily lives. As a matter of fact, AI has changed us lives in many ways. Some people worry that it will take away jobs and make us lazy, while others believe that AI can improve our lives. In my opinion, AI has both advantages and disadvantages. It depends on how we use it.

  On the one hand, AI helps us study and work more efficiently. For example, translation apps and writing assistants can save us time. On the other hand, if we rely on AI too much, we may lose the ability to think independently.

  As far as I am concerned, AI can make our lives better. We should use it wisely and keep thinking independently.
```

```text
  Food safety problem has been bothering us, and now they have became a serious problem for many people. What are the causes of food safety problem?
  
  In my opinion, there are two main causes. First, some shops may sell old or bad food. If people eat this kind of food, they may become ill. Second, some shops care more about making money than food quality. To solve this problem, the government should make laws and regulate shops more carefully. In addition, the mass media should publicize food safety and let people know the importance of food safety.

  Of course, there is still a long way to go before we solve this problem. We should continue to find new ways to solve it.
```
### 方法型

如何保持健康

```text
  It is well known that health is very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, a healthy body and a clear mind can help us live a better life. In my opinion, everyone should try to keep healthy. There are many ways to do this.

  First of all, we should eat healthy food, such as vegetables and fruit. Second, we should do more exercise. For example, we can run, walk or play basketball after work or study. A healthy body can give us enouth energy for work and study. In addition, it is important to get enough sleep every day. We should not stay up too late.

  As far as I am concerned, health is important and necessary for everyone.
```

### 个人观点型

我的爱好

```text
  It is well known that music is very popular and trendy in our lives. As a matter of fact, music is good for us in many ways. Some people enjoy dancing, while others enjoy listening to music. As for me, my hobby is listening to music. Here are the reasons.

  First of all, it is obvious that music can make me happy. For example, I often listen to my favorite songs before going to bed. In addition, there is no doubt that music can help me relax after a busy day. Last but not least, it goes without saying that music can make my life more meaningful.

  As far as I am concerned, listening to music is important, helpful to me.
```
