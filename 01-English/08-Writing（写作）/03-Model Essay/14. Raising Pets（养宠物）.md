# 14. Raising Pets（养宠物）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 你喜欢养宠物吗？说明理由。

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that pets are very popular in our lives. As a matter of fact, raising pets is good for us in many ways. Some people like raising pets, while others do not. As for me, I like raising pets. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, raising pets can make me happy and reduce my stress. In addition, raising pets can help me relax. Last but not least, if I raise a pet, I can spend more time with my family and friends, which is very meaningful.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, I like raising pets.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，宠物在我们的生活中很流行。事实上，养宠物在很多方面对我们有好处。一些人喜欢养宠物，另一些人不喜欢。对我来说，我喜欢养宠物。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，养宠物可以让我快乐、减轻压力。此外，养宠物可以帮助我放松。最后，如果我养宠物，我可以花更多时间陪伴家人和朋友，这很有意义。  
> 
> 因此，就我而言，我喜欢养宠物。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（背景+对比引入）**：`... pets are very popular in our lives. Some people like ..., while others do not. As for me, ...`
- **主体论证（情绪抚慰+心理放松+增进陪伴）**：
  - 减压愉悦：`make me happy and reduce my stress.`
  - 心理放松：`help me relax.`
  - 家庭陪伴纽带：`spend more time with my family and friends, which is very meaningful.`
- **末段总结**：`As a result, as far as I am concerned, I like raising pets.`
