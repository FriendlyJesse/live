# 03. My Hobby（我的爱好）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 你的爱好是什么？为什么有这个爱好？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that hobbies are very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, hobbies are good for us in many ways. Some people like dancing, while others like reading. As for me, my hobby is playing football. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, playing football can help me keep fit, and everyone wants to live a healthy life. In addition, playing football can help me relax and reduce my stress. Last but not least, playing football can make me happy. I can make friends when playing football, which enriches my social life.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, playing football is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，爱好在我们的生活中很重要。事实上，爱好在很多方面对我们有好处。一些人喜欢跳舞，另一些人喜欢阅读。对我来说，我的爱好是踢足球。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，踢足球可以帮助我保持健康，每个人都希望过健康的生活。此外，踢足球可以帮助我放松、减轻压力。最后，踢足球可以让我快乐。我能在踢足球时交朋友，这丰富了我的社交生活。  
> 
> 因此，在我看来，踢足球很重要、有帮助且绝对必要。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段引出**：`Some people like ..., while others like ... As for me, my hobby is...`
- **主体展开（健康+心理+社交）**：
  - 身体健康：`... help me keep fit, and everyone wants to live a healthy life.`
  - 心理减压：`... help me relax and reduce my stress.`
  - 社交与快乐：`... make me happy / I can make friends when ..., which enriches my social life.`（非限制性定语从句）
- **末段三段式排比总结**：`... is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.`
