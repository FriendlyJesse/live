# 17. Volunteer Activity Email（给 Tom 的志愿活动邮件）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 给 Tom 写邮件，分享暑假参加社区志愿活动的经历和感受。

## 📝 英文范文
> Dear Tom,  
> 
> I am writing to tell you that I took part in a volunteer activity during the summer vacation. I visited old people who lived alone. I brought them some fruit and flowers, talked with them, and helped them cook dinner. After dinner, I read newspapers to them. They were very happy.  
> 
> Working as a volunteer is very meaningful. First of all, it helps me realize my self-worth by helping others. In addition, it makes me happy and teaches me many new things. Last but not least, I made friends with other volunteers, which enriches my social life.  
> 
> I am looking forward to meeting you. I wish you and your family good health and happiness.  
> 
> Yours,  
> Like

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 亲爱的 Tom：  
> 
> 我写信是想告诉你，我暑假参加了一个志愿活动。我去看望了独居老人，给他们带了水果和鲜花，陪他们聊天，并帮助他们做晚饭。晚饭后，我为他们读报纸。他们非常高兴。  
> 
> 作为志愿者是很有意义的。首先，通过帮助他人，它让我实现自我价值。此外，它使我快乐，并教会我许多新东西。最后，我还和其他志愿者交了朋友，这丰富了我的社交生活。  
> 
> 我期待与你见面。祝你和家人健康幸福。  
> 
> 你的，  
> Like

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（经历陈述与具体动作）**：
  - 阐明目的：`I am writing to tell you that I took part in a volunteer activity...`
  - 细节描写：`visited old people ... brought fruit and flowers, talked with them, helped cook dinner, read newspapers...`
- **第二段（感受与意义升华）**：
  - 自我价值：`helps me realize my self-worth by helping others.`
  - 学习新知：`makes me happy and teaches me many new things.`
  - 拓展社交：`made friends with other volunteers, which enriches my social life.`
- **末段祝福**：`I am looking forward to meeting you. I wish you and your family good health and happiness.`
