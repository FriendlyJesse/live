# 13. My Favorite Chinese Festival（我最喜欢的中国节日）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 你最喜爱的中国节日是什么？为什么喜欢它？

## 📝 英文范文
> My favorite Chinese festival is the Spring Festival, which is also known as the Chinese New Year. We can do many things during the holiday, and it makes us happy. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, during the Chinese New Year, people have parties and enjoy many kinds of delicious food. In addition, the Chinese New Year lasts for 15 days, and people can meet many friends, which enriches our social life. Last but not least, people sing, dance, and play games during the holiday.  
> 
> As a result, the Spring Festival is my favorite Chinese festival.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 我最喜欢的中国节日是春节，它也被称为中国新年。假期中我们可以做很多事，它让我们快乐。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，春节期间，人们举行聚会，享受各种美食。此外，春节持续 15 天，人们可以见到许多朋友，这丰富了我们的社交生活。最后，人们会在假期唱歌、跳舞和玩游戏。  
> 
> 因此，春节是我最喜欢的中国节日。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（节日定名+同位语从句）**：`... the Spring Festival, which is also known as the Chinese New Year.`
- **主体节日习俗描述**：
  - 聚会美食：`have parties and enjoy many kinds of delicious food.`
  - 走亲访友：`lasts for 15 days, and people can meet many friends, which enriches our social life.`
  - 庆祝活动：`sing, dance, and play games during the holiday.`
- **末段总结**：`As a result, the Spring Festival is my favorite Chinese festival.`
