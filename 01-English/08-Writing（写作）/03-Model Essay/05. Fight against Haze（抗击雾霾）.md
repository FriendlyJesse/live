# 05. Fight against Haze（抗击雾霾）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 雾霾有哪些危害？我们可以采取哪些措施？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that clean air is very important in our lives. Haze can lead to serious diseases such as lung cancer. In addition, haze may lead to traffic accidents, and it is harmful to the environment.  
> 
> Here are the things we should do. First of all, we should choose public transportation to reduce resource waste and environmental pollution. In addition, the government should shut down heavily polluting factories and make laws to protect the environment. Last but not least, we should plant more trees because trees can help clean the air.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, these measures are necessary to fight against haze.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，清洁的空气在我们的生活中很重要。雾霾会导致严重疾病，例如肺癌。此外，雾霾可能导致交通事故，而且会危害环境。  
> 
> 以下是我们应该做的事。首先，我们应选择公共交通，以减少资源浪费和环境污染。此外，政府应关闭重污染工厂，并制定法律保护环境。最后，我们应该多种树，因为树能帮助净化空气。  
> 
> 因此，在我看来，这些措施对于抗击雾霾是必要的。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（背景+危害双阐述）**：
  - 疾病危害：`... lead to serious diseases such as lung cancer.`
  - 交通与环境：`... lead to traffic accidents, and it is harmful to the environment.`
- **第二段（三方具体措施）**：
  - 个人层面：`choose public transportation to reduce resource waste and environmental pollution.`
  - 政府层面：`shut down heavily polluting factories and make laws to protect the environment.`
  - 生态层面：`plant more trees because trees can help clean the air.`
- **末段总结**：`... these measures are necessary to fight against haze.`
