# 15. Start Your Own Business or Find a Job（创业还是就业）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 毕业后你选择创业还是找工作？为什么？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that work is very important in our lives. Some people want to start their own businesses after graduation, while others want to find a job. As for me, I will choose to start my own business after graduation. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, starting my own business can teach me many new things. In addition, it can help me realize my self-worth. Last but not least, if I start my own business, I can spend more time with my family and friends, and I am free to do many other things.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, I will choose to start my own business after graduation.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，工作在我们的生活中很重要。一些人毕业后想自己创业，另一些人想找工作。对我来说，我会选择毕业后创业。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，创业可以教会我许多新东西。此外，它可以帮助我实现自我价值。最后，如果我创业，我可以花更多时间陪伴家人和朋友，也可以自由地做许多其他事情。  
> 
> 因此，在我看来，我会选择毕业后创业。
