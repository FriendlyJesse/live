# 02. E-books or Paper Books（电子书还是纸质书）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 你喜欢电子书还是纸质书？理由是什么？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that books are very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, reading is good for us in many ways. Some people like e-books, while others like paper books. As for me, I prefer e-books to paper books. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, e-books can save me money because paper books are expensive. In addition, e-books are convenient to carry and can save a lot of trouble. Last but not least, e-books are good for the environment. People should choose e-books to reduce resource waste and environmental pollution.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, e-books are better than paper books.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，书在我们的生活中很重要。事实上，阅读在很多方面对我们有好处。一些人喜欢电子书，另一些人喜欢纸质书。对我来说，比起纸质书，我更喜欢电子书。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，电子书可以为我省钱，因为纸质书很贵。此外，电子书携带方便，可以省去很多麻烦。最后，电子书对环境有好处。人们应该选择电子书来减少资源浪费和环境污染。  
> 
> 因此，在我看来，电子书比纸质书更好。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段引出与对比**：`Some people like ..., while others like ...` + `As for me, I prefer A to B.`（对比引入观点）
- **主体展开（三维论证）**：
  - 经济成本：`... can save me money because ... are expensive.`
  - 便利性：`... are convenient to carry and can save a lot of trouble.`
  - 环保效益：`... good for the environment / reduce resource waste and environmental pollution.`
- **末段结论**：`As a result, as far as I am concerned, A is better than B.`
