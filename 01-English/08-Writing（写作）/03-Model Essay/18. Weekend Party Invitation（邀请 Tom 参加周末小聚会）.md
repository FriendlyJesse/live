# 18. Weekend Party Invitation（邀请 Tom 参加周末小聚会）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 邀请 Tom 参加 Lucy 的生日晚会，写明时间、地点，并询问他能否参加。

## 📝 英文范文
> Dear Tom,  
> 
> I am writing this email to invite you to join Lucy's birthday party. The party will take place at Lucy's house. It will begin at 7:00 p.m. on Sunday. You can make some friends at the party, which will enrich your social life. We will have a great dinner together. After dinner, we will sing, dance, and play games. The party will last for about three hours, from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.  
> 
> I really hope you can come and join us. Would you like to come? I am looking forward to your early reply. I wish you and your family good health and happiness.  
> 
> Yours,  
> Like

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 亲爱的 Tom：  
> 
> 我写这封邮件是想邀请你参加 Lucy 的生日晚会。聚会将在 Lucy 家举行，周日晚上 7 点开始。你可以在聚会上交一些朋友，这会丰富你的社交生活。我们将一起享用丰盛晚餐。晚餐后，我们会唱歌、跳舞和玩游戏。聚会大约持续 3 小时，从晚上 7 点到 10 点。  
> 
> 我真心希望你能来参加。你愿意来吗？我期待你的早日回复。祝你和家人健康幸福。  
> 
> 你的，  
> Like

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（发出邀请+时间地点要素）**：
  - 阐明邀请：`I am writing this email to invite you to join...`
  - 地点要素：`The party will take place at Lucy's house.`
  - 时间要素：`It will begin at 7:00 p.m. on Sunday. ... will last for about three hours, from 7:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.`
- **活动细节安排**：
  - 社交：`make some friends ... which will enrich your social life.`
  - 聚餐与娱乐：`have a great dinner ... sing, dance, and play games.`
- **结尾诚意表达与问询**：
  - `I really hope you can come and join us. Would you like to come?`
  - `I am looking forward to your early reply.`
