# 16. Travel Advice Email（给 Mike 的旅游建议信）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 给来家乡旅游的 Mike 写邮件，说明近期天气、注意事项，并表达期待见面。

## 📝 英文范文
> Dear Mike,  
> 
> I heard that you will visit my hometown soon. It is going to be sunny next week, and there is usually a gentle wind in the afternoon these days.  
> 
> I am writing to give you some travel advice. First of all, you can bring some light and comfortable clothes with you. In addition, you should take care of your belongings and use official transportation because it is important to stay safe. Last but not least, my hometown, Beijing, has many interesting places, such as the Great Wall, the Forbidden City, and the Summer Palace. You can spend two or three days visiting these sights.  
> 
> I am looking forward to meeting you. I wish you and your family good health and happiness.  
> 
> Yours,  
> Like

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 亲爱的 Mike：  
> 
> 我听说你很快要来我的家乡旅游。下周将会是晴天，这几天下午通常有微风。  
> 
> 我写信是想给你一些旅游建议。首先，你可以带一些轻便舒适的衣服。此外，你应该照看好随身物品并使用正规交通工具，因为安全很重要。最后，我的家乡北京有许多有趣的地方，如长城、故宫和颐和园。你可以花两三天游览这些景点。  
> 
> 我期待与你见面。祝你和家人健康幸福。  
> 
> 你的，  
> Like

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **称呼与寒暄**：`Dear [Name],` + `I heard that you will visit my hometown soon.`
- **天气描述**：`It is going to be sunny next week, and there is usually a gentle wind in the afternoon these days.`
- **写信目的与建议（三点）**：
  - 衣物准备：`bring some light and comfortable clothes with you.`
  - 安全与出行：`take care of your belongings and use official transportation because it is important to stay safe.`
  - 景点推荐：`has many interesting places, such as A, B, and C. You can spend two or three days visiting these sights.`
- **结尾与祝愿套句**：
  - `I am looking forward to meeting you.`
  - `I wish you and your family good health and happiness.`
