# 11. Spring Is Coming（春天来了）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 描写春天的景象，并介绍人们在春天可以进行的活动。

## 📝 英文范文
> Spring is coming. It is a beautiful and comfortable season. You can see flowers and trees everywhere. It is often sunny in spring, and the weather is neither too cold nor too hot. Birds sing in the sky, and little animals also come out.  
> 
> There are many things we can do in spring. First of all, we can have a party with our friends, play football together, and chat with each other. In addition, we can do meaningful things. For example, we can visit old people who live alone, talk with them, and cook dinner for them. Last but not least, I can spend more time with my family and friends in spring.  
> 
> In a word, spring is my favorite season.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 春天来了。这是一个美丽舒适的季节。你可以看到到处都有鲜花和树木。春天常常阳光明媚，天气既不太冷也不太热。鸟儿在天空中歌唱，小动物也出来活动。  
> 
> 春天我们可以做很多事。首先，我们可以和朋友聚会、一起踢足球、互相聊天。此外，我们还可以做有意义的事。例如，我们可以看望独居老人，和他们聊天，并为他们做饭。最后，在春天我可以花更多时间陪伴家人和朋友。  
> 
> 总之，春天是我最喜欢的季节。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（自然景物生动描写）**：
  - 天气气候：`beautiful and comfortable ... neither too cold nor too hot.`
  - 动植物景致：`flowers and trees everywhere ... Birds sing ... little animals come out.`
- **主体（丰富多彩的春季活动）**：
  - 朋友聚会娱乐：`have a party with our friends, play football together, and chat with each other.`
  - 志愿关爱活动：`visit old people who live alone, talk with them, and cook dinner for them.`
  - 亲友陪伴时光：`spend more time with my family and friends.`
- **末段总结**：`In a word, spring is my favorite season.`
