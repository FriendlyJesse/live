# 04. I Like Playing Sports（我喜欢运动）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 你经常做什么运动？体育运动有哪些好处？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that playing sports is very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, sports are good for us in many ways. As for me, I like playing football most. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, playing football can help me keep fit, and everyone wants to live a healthy life. In addition, playing football can help me relax and reduce my stress. Last but not least, playing football can make me happy. I can make friends when playing football, which enriches my social life.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, playing football is my favorite sport.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，体育运动在我们的生活中很重要。事实上，运动在很多方面对我们有好处。对我来说，我最喜欢踢足球。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，踢足球可以帮助我保持健康，每个人都希望过健康的生活。此外，踢足球可以帮助我放松、减轻压力。最后，踢足球能让我快乐，我能在踢足球时交朋友，这丰富了我的社交生活。  
> 
> 因此，就我而言，踢足球是我最喜欢的运动。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段引出**：`... is very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, ... are good for us in many ways.`
- **主体论证核心点**：
  - 强身健体：`help me keep fit`
  - 舒缓压力：`help me relax and reduce my stress`
  - 增进友谊：`make friends ... which enriches my social life`
- **末段总结**：`As a result, as far as I am concerned, ... is my favorite sport.`
