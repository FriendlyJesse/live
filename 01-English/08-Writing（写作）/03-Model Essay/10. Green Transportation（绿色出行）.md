# 10. Green Transportation（绿色出行）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 你怎样理解绿色出行？你会选择什么绿色方式？理由是什么？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that green transportation is becoming popular in our lives. As a matter of fact, green transportation is good for us in many ways. As for me, it is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.  
> 
> I choose to ride a bike in my daily life. First of all, riding a bike helps me keep fit and makes me strong. In addition, riding a bike can save me money because taking a taxi is expensive. Last but not least, riding a bike can save a lot of trouble, and it is very meaningful.  
> 
> As far as I am concerned, riding a bike is very important for saving energy.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，绿色出行在我们的生活中越来越流行。事实上，绿色出行在很多方面对我们有好处。对我来说，它很重要、有帮助且绝对必要。  
> 
> 在日常生活中，我选择骑自行车。首先，骑自行车能帮助我保持健康，使我强壮。此外，骑自行车能为我省钱，因为坐出租车很贵。最后，骑自行车可以省去很多麻烦，也很有意义。  
> 
> 在我看来，骑自行车对于节约能源非常重要。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段引出**：`... is becoming popular in our lives. ... is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.`
- **主体论证（健身+省钱+省心有意义）**：
  - 强健身体：`... helps me keep fit and makes me strong.`
  - 节省花费：`... can save me money because taking a taxi is expensive.`
  - 减少麻烦与意义：`... can save a lot of trouble, and it is very meaningful.`
- **末段总结**：`... riding a bike is very important for saving energy.`
