# 12. An Unforgettable Holiday（难忘的一个假期）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 哪一个假期令你难忘？为什么？

## 📝 英文范文
> Last summer, I had an unforgettable holiday. I took a trip to Beijing. There are many interesting places in Beijing, such as the Great Wall, the Forbidden City, and the Summer Palace. I spent three days visiting these sights.  
> 
> Here are the reasons why I will never forget the holiday. First of all, the local people were very friendly and kind. I made a lot of friends there, which enriched my social life. In addition, I enjoyed many kinds of delicious food. Last but not least, the holiday made me happy. It helped me relax and reduce my stress.  
> 
> As a result, as far as I am concerned, the summer holiday was an unforgettable experience.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 去年夏天，我度过了一个难忘的假期。我去北京旅行了。北京有许多有趣的地方，如长城、故宫和颐和园。我花了三天时间游览这些景点。  
> 
> 以下是我永远不会忘记这个假期的原因。首先，当地人非常友好善良。我在那里交了很多朋友，这丰富了我的社交生活。此外，我享用了许多种美食。最后，这个假期使我快乐，它帮助我放松并减轻压力。  
> 
> 因此，就我而言，这个暑假是一次难忘的经历。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（时间+地点+景点列举）**：`Last summer, I took a trip to... There are many interesting places in ..., such as A, B, and C.`
- **主体论证（人文+美食+心情）**：
  - 当地人文：`the local people were very friendly and kind. I made a lot of friends...`
  - 美食体验：`enjoyed many kinds of delicious food.`
  - 减压愉悦：`helped me relax and reduce my stress.`
- **末段总结**：`... was an unforgettable experience.`
