# 09. Live a Healthy Life（过健康的生活）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 什么是健康的生活方式？你是怎么做的？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that a healthy life is very important. As a matter of fact, a healthy lifestyle is good for us in many ways. Everyone hopes to live a healthy life. As for me, a healthy life means a balanced diet, exercise, and good sleep. Here are the things we should do.  
> 
> First of all, we should eat healthy food; a balanced diet can make us strong. In addition, doing exercise can help us relax and reduce stress. Last but not least, I always go to bed and get up on time.  
> 
> As far as I am concerned, a healthy life is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，健康的生活很重要。事实上，健康的生活方式在很多方面对我们有好处。每个人都希望过健康的生活。对我来说，健康的生活意味着均衡饮食、锻炼和良好睡眠。以下是我们应该做的事。  
> 
> 首先，我们应该吃健康食物；均衡饮食能使我们强壮。此外，锻炼可以帮助我们放松、减轻压力。最后，我总是按时睡觉和起床。  
> 
> 在我看来，健康的生活很重要、有帮助且绝对必要。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（下定义+拆解概念）**：`As for me, a healthy life means a balanced diet, exercise, and good sleep.`
- **主体行动论述（饮食+运动+作息）**：
  - 饮食：`... eat healthy food; a balanced diet can make us strong.`
  - 运动：`... doing exercise can help us relax and reduce stress.`
  - 作息：`... always go to bed and get up on time.`
- **末段总结**：`... a healthy life is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.`
