# 07. Exercise Every Day（每天锻炼）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 坚持锻炼有哪些好处？你能否坚持每天锻炼？

## 📝 英文范文
> It is well known that exercise is very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, doing exercise is good for us in many ways. Some people like swimming, while others like cycling. As for me, my favorite sport is playing football. Here are the reasons.  
> 
> First of all, playing football can help me keep fit, and everyone wants to live a healthy life. In addition, playing football can help me relax and reduce my stress. Last but not least, playing football can make me happy. I can make friends when playing football, which enriches my social life.  
> 
> As far as I am concerned, exercise is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 众所周知，锻炼在我们的生活中很重要。事实上，锻炼在很多方面对我们有好处。一些人喜欢游泳，另一些人喜欢骑自行车。对我来说，我最喜欢的运动是踢足球。原因如下。  
> 
> 首先，踢足球可以帮助我保持健康，每个人都希望过健康的生活。此外，踢足球可以帮助我放松和减轻压力。最后，踢足球可以让我快乐；我能在踢足球时交朋友，这丰富了我的社交生活。  
> 
> 在我看来，锻炼很重要、有帮助且绝对必要。

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段引出**：`It is well known that ... is very important in our lives. As a matter of fact, ... is good for us in many ways.`
- **论据层层递进**：
  - 身体益处：`help me keep fit, and everyone wants to live a healthy life.`
  - 心理调节：`help me relax and reduce my stress.`
  - 社交丰富：`make friends ... which enriches my social life.`
- **经典三词强总结**：`... is important, helpful, and absolutely necessary.`
