# 19. Introduce a Chinese Festival Email（给 John 回信介绍中国传统节日）

## 📌 写作要求
> **题目：** 给 John 回邮件，介绍一个中国传统节日，包括节日名称、由来和庆祝方式。

## 📝 英文范文
> Dear John,  
> 
> I am writing to introduce a traditional Chinese festival to you: the Chinese New Year, also known as the Spring Festival. In ancient times, there was a monster named Nian. It was a dangerous animal and was afraid of the color red. To drive it away, people put up red paper on their windows and doors.  
> 
> First of all, during the Chinese New Year, people have parties and enjoy many kinds of delicious food. In addition, the Chinese New Year lasts for 15 days, and we can meet many friends, which enriches our social life. Last but not least, people sing, dance, and play games during the holiday.  
> 
> I wish you and your family good health and happiness. I am looking forward to your early reply.  
> 
> Yours,  
> Like

## 🇨🇳 中文译文
> 亲爱的 John：  
> 
> 我写信是想向你介绍一个中国传统节日——中国新年，也就是春节。在古代，有一只叫“年”的怪兽。它是一种危险的动物，而且害怕红色。为了赶走年兽，人们在门窗上贴红纸。  
> 
> 首先，春节期间，人们会举行聚会并享用各种美食。此外，春节持续 15 天，我们可以见到许多朋友，这丰富了我们的社交生活。最后，人们会在假期唱歌、跳舞和玩游戏。  
> 
> 祝你和家人健康幸福。我期待你的早日回复。  
> 
> 你的，  
> Like

## 💡 句式拆解与亮点词句
- **首段（节日引出+神话传说由来）**：
  - 阐明主题：`I am writing to introduce a traditional Chinese festival to you: ...`
  - 传说背景：`In ancient times, there was a monster named Nian. It was a dangerous animal and was afraid of the color red.`
  - 习俗由来：`To drive it away, people put up red paper on their windows and doors.`
- **第二段（三项传统庆祝方式）**：
  - 美食盛宴：`have parties and enjoy many kinds of delicious food.`
  - 走亲访友：`lasts for 15 days, and we can meet many friends...`
  - 联欢娱乐：`sing, dance, and play games during the holiday.`
- **末段祝福与期盼**：`I wish you and your family good health and happiness. I am looking forward to your early reply.`
