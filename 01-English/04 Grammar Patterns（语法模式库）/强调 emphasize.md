# 强调

## 感叹词

**Wow**, rabbits like carrots!
**Oh my god**, rabbit like carrots!
**Bloody hell**, rabbits like carrots!

## 词汇强调

### 强调句

结构：
`It + be（is / was）+ 被强调部分 + that / who + 句子其余部分`

👉 本质：把某一部分“拎出来强调”
👉 判断方法：  
去掉 **It is/was...that**，句子仍然成立

---

原句：

The rabbit will eat the carrot.

强调主语：

It is the rabbit that will eat the carrot.

---

原句：

I met a lovely rabbit in the field this morning.
我今早在田里遇到了一只可爱的兔子。

强调主语：
It was I who met a lovely rabbit in the field this morning.

强调宾语：
It was a lovely rabbit that I met in the field this morning.

强调地点：
It was in the field that I met a lovely rabbit this morning.

强调时间：
It was this morning that I met a lovely rabbit in the field.

---

You are so careless!
你真粗心！

强调主语：
It is you who are so careless!

### 助动词

👉 一句话：
do / does / did = 强调“确实做了”

结构：
do / does / did + 动词原形

---

例句：

Rabbits **do** like carrots!
兔子确实喜欢胡萝卜！

I did meet a lovely rabbit.
我**确实**见到了一只兔子。

### [[倒装 inversion]]

👉 一句话：  
**为了强调，把正常语序“倒过来”**

| 原句                                     | 倒装句                                      | 意思         |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------- |
| You go off!                            | Off you go!                              | 你去吧        |
| The wolf comes(here)!                  | Here comes the wolf!                     | 狼来了！       |
| I have never met a wolf.               | Never have I met a wolf.                 | 我从没遇见过狼。   |
| I did not eat single carrot yesterday. | Not a single carrot did I eat yesterday. | 我昨天没有吃胡萝卜。 |

### 感叹句

👉 一句话：  
**What / How = 强调情绪（惊讶/赞美）**

What 结构（名词）：
What + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词！

How 结构（形容词）：
How + 形容词 + 主语 + 谓语！

| 原句                         | 感叹句                             | 意思           |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------ |
| The carrot is big!         | How big the carrot is!          | 这胡萝卜真大！      |
| You've grown a big carrot! | What a big carrot you've grown! | 你种了一根好大的胡萝卜！ |

### 重复某个词

He went on and on and on.
他说个不停。

He walked and walked and walked.
他走个不停。

### 强调代词

I can do it **myself**!
我自己能做。

I **myself** went there!
我本人去了那里！

上面两个是表强调，而下面那个则是普通的反身代词：

I saw myself in the mirror.
我在镜子里看见自己。

### 副词强调

really adv.
核心：强调真实性 → “真的”

Rabbits really like carrots!
兔子真的喜欢胡萝卜！

---

very adv.
核心：放大程度 → “非常”

very + adj/adv
very fast = 非常快

the very + 名词
= 正是那个最...
the very beginning = 最开始

I eat very fast! / I eat very, very, very fast!
我吃东西很快！

I missed the **very**（修饰名词，最 ...的） beginning of the movie.
我错过了电影刚刚开始的那一段。

---

just adv.
核心：距离很近/完全匹配

this is just unbelievable!
这绝对难以置信！

---

ever adv. 曾经
核心：任何时间范围

All I ever wanted is that carrot.
我所要的就是那根胡萝卜。

---

in the world/on earth

What in the world/on earth are you talking about?
你究竟在说什么？
