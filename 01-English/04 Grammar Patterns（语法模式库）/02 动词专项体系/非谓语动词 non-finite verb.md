# 非谓语动词（Non-finite Verbs）

## 什么是非谓语动词

非谓语动词是不能独立充当句子谓语的动词形式（参见 [[动词分类 verb Classification]] 与 [[英语语法综述 grammar Overview]]）。它不随主语的人称和数变化，也通常不直接表示句子的时态。

英语的非谓语动词主要有三类：

| 类型 | 常见形式 | 核心含义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 动词不定式 | `to do` | 将来、目的、具体动作 |
| 动名词 | `doing` | 动作本身、习惯、用途 |
| 分词 | `doing` / `done` | 主动进行、被动完成、状态 |

```text
The rabbit eats a carrot.
The rabbit ate a carrot.
The rabbit is eating a carrot.
The rabbit has eaten a carrot.
```

上面各句中的 `eats`、`ate`、`is eating`、`has eaten` 都能作谓语，且会随时态变化，因此属于谓语动词。

```text
I like to eat carrots.
I liked eating carrots.
I am a talking rabbit.
The rabbit likes minced carrots.
```

- `to eat`：动词不定式，作宾语。
- `eating`：动名词，作宾语。
- `talking`：现在分词，作定语。
- `minced`：过去分词，作定语。

## 谓语动词与非谓语动词

一个简单句通常只能有一个谓语动词。

```text
❌ I am a rabbit, I eat carrots.
```

上面有两个谓语动词：`am` 和 `eat`，不能只用逗号连接。

可以改为并列句：

```text
I am a rabbit, so I eat carrots.
我是兔子，所以我吃胡萝卜。
```

也可以改为复合句：

```text
Because I am a rabbit, I eat carrots.
因为我是兔子，所以我吃胡萝卜。
```

还可以把其中一个动作改成非谓语形式：

```text
Being a rabbit, I eat carrots.
作为一只兔子，我吃胡萝卜。
```

## 动词不定式

### 基本形式

```text
to + 动词原形
```

```text
to eat
to study
to be
to have
```

```text
To be or not to be, that is the question.
生存还是毁灭，这是一个问题。
```

有时 `to` 可以省略，例如使役动词、感官动词后的不定式。

```text
make / let / have + sb + do
see / hear / watch + sb + do
help + sb + (to) do
```

```text
The rabbit made the wolf laugh.
兔子把狼逗笑了。

The wolf saw the rabbit eat a carrot.
狼看见兔子吃了一根胡萝卜。

The wolf helped the rabbit (to) grow carrots.
狼帮助兔子种胡萝卜。
```

### 不定式作主语

```text
To eat a carrot every day is good for the rabbit.
每天吃一根胡萝卜对兔子有好处。
```

较长的不定式短语作主语时，常用 `it` 作形式主语：

```text
It is good for the rabbit to eat a carrot every day.
每天吃一根胡萝卜对兔子有好处。
```

也可以改写成主语从句：

```text
It is good that the rabbit eats a carrot every day.
兔子每天吃一根胡萝卜是有好处的。
```

### 不定式作宾语

```text
The rabbit likes to eat carrots.
兔子喜欢吃胡萝卜。
```

常见后接不定式作宾语的动词：

```text
want, hope, decide, plan, agree, choose, learn,
expect, promise, refuse, manage, afford, pretend
```

```text
I decided to study English every day.
我决定每天学习英语。
```

### 不定式作宾语补足语

```text
主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 + to do
```

```text
The rabbit expected the wolf to eat a carrot.
兔子指望狼吃一根胡萝卜。
```

```text
I consider it important to eat a carrot every day.
我认为每天吃一根胡萝卜很重要。
```

常见结构：

```text
ask / tell / want / invite / encourage / allow / expect + sb + to do
```

注意：使役动词和部分感官动词后通常用动词原形，而不是 `to do`。

```text
make / let / have + sb + do
see / hear / watch + sb + do
```

```text
✅ The rabbit made the wolf laugh.
❌ The rabbit made the wolf to laugh.
```

### 不定式作表语

```text
The rabbit's dream is to eat every kind of carrot in the world.
兔子的梦想是吃遍世界上的每一种胡萝卜。
```

```text
To eat every kind of carrot in the world is the rabbit's dream.
吃遍世界上的每一种胡萝卜是兔子的梦想。
```

### 不定式作定语

```text
The rabbit has a lot of carrots to eat.
兔子有很多胡萝卜要吃。
```

`to eat` 修饰 `carrots`，作后置定语。

```text
I have a lot of work to do.
我有很多工作要做。
```

### 不定式作状语

不定式常作状语，表示原因、目的或结果。

#### 表原因

```text
I was surprised to get a thumbs-up.
我很吃惊，因为得到了一个赞。
```

相当于：

```text
I was surprised because I got a thumbs-up.
```

#### 表目的

```text
I will do anything to get a thumbs-up.
我会做任何事来获得一个赞。
```

相当于：

```text
I will do anything in order that I get a thumbs-up.
```

更常见的目的表达：

```text
I will do anything in order to get a thumbs-up.
I will do anything so as to get a thumbs-up.
```

#### 表结果

```text
I got enough thumbs-up to make another video.
我得到了足够多的赞，可以再制作一个视频。
```

### 不定式的完成式和进行式

不定式可以结合不同形式，表示不同的时间关系。

| 形式 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `to do` | 一般、将来或与谓语动作同时 | `The rabbit seems to eat carrots.` |
| `to be doing` | 正在进行 | `The rabbit seems to be eating a carrot.` |
| `to have done` | 动作先于谓语 | `The rabbit appears to have eaten a carrot.` |
| `to be done` | 被动 | `The work needs to be finished.` |
| `to have been done` | 完成被动 | `The work seems to have been finished.` |

```text
The rabbit seemed to be eating a carrot.
兔子当时似乎正在吃一根胡萝卜。

The rabbit appears to have eaten a carrot.
兔子看起来已经吃过一根胡萝卜。
```

## 动名词

### 基本形式

```text
动词原形 + -ing
```

```text
eat → eating
grow → growing
sleep → sleeping
```

动名词形式与现在分词相同，但功能不同：

- 动名词更像名词，表示动作、习惯、用途或抽象概念。
- 现在分词更像形容词，表示主动、进行或状态。

### 动名词作主语

```text
Carrots are healthy for the rabbit.
胡萝卜对兔子很健康。

Eating carrots is healthy for the rabbit.
吃胡萝卜对兔子很健康。
```

```text
Seeing is believing.
眼见为实。
```

### 动名词作表语

```text
The rabbit's hobby is growing carrots.
兔子的爱好是种胡萝卜。
```

### 动名词作定语

动名词作定语通常表示用途、功能或类别。

```text
a sleeping pill
安眠药

a pill for sleeping
用于睡眠的药
```

对比：

```text
a sleeping rabbit
正在睡觉的兔子
```

- `sleeping` 是现在分词，表示兔子正在进行的动作。
- `sleeping` 在 `a sleeping pill` 中是动名词，表示药的用途。

### 动名词作宾语

```text
The rabbit likes eating carrots.
兔子喜欢吃胡萝卜。
```

部分动词后通常接动名词，而不接不定式：

```text
enjoy, avoid, admit, advise, consider, escape,
finish, mind, practice, suggest, keep, imagine
```

```text
The rabbit enjoys eating carrots.
兔子喜欢吃胡萝卜。
```

```text
✅ The rabbit enjoys eating carrots.
❌ The rabbit enjoys to eat carrots.
```

介词后通常也接动名词：

```text
The rabbit is fond of eating carrots.
兔子喜欢吃胡萝卜。

The rabbit is looking forward to seeing the wolf again.
兔子期待再次见到狼。
```

注意：`look forward to` 中的 `to` 是介词，因此后面用 `doing`，不是不定式。

### `to do` 与 `doing` 意义不同

有些动词后接不定式或动名词，句意会明显变化。

| 结构 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `stop to do` | 停下手头的事，去做另一件事 | `The rabbit stopped to eat a carrot.` |
| `stop doing` | 停止正在进行的事 | `The rabbit stopped eating a carrot.` |
| `forget to do` | 忘记去做某事，事情未做 | `The wolf forgot to invite the rabbit.` |
| `forget doing` | 忘记做过某事，事情已做 | `The rabbit forgot accepting the invitation.` |

```text
The rabbit stopped to eat a carrot.
兔子停下手头的事，去吃一根胡萝卜。

The rabbit stopped eating a carrot.
兔子停止吃胡萝卜。
```

```text
The wolf forgot to invite the rabbit to her party.
狼忘记邀请兔子参加她的聚会。

The rabbit forgot accepting the wolf's invitation.
兔子忘记了自己曾接受狼的邀请。
```

## 现在分词

### 基本形式

```text
动词原形 + -ing
```

现在分词不一定表示“现在”，它的核心意义是主动、进行或伴随。

### 现在分词作定语

```text
a talking rabbit
一只正在说话的兔子

a rabbit for talking
用于说话的兔子
```

- `talking` 作现在分词定语，说明兔子正在做的动作或具有的特征。
- `for talking` 表示用途，含义不同。

```text
The rabbit is charming.
兔子很有魅力。

The story is interesting.
故事很有趣。

The game is exciting.
游戏令人兴奋。

The news is encouraging.
这个消息令人鼓舞。
```

现在分词作表语时，常表示“令人……的”，描述事物本身具有的性质。

```text
interesting 令人感兴趣的
exciting    令人兴奋的
encouraging 令人受到鼓舞的
```

### 现在分词作宾语补足语

```text
The wolf saw the rabbit eating a carrot.
狼看见兔子正在吃一根胡萝卜。
```

```text
see / hear / watch / notice + sb + doing
```

表示看到、听到某个动作正在进行。

对比：

```text
The wolf saw the rabbit eat a carrot.
狼看见兔子吃了一根胡萝卜。
```

```text
see + sb + do
```

表示看到了动作的全过程或完整事件。

### 现在分词作状语

现在分词短语可作时间、原因、条件、方式、结果或伴随状语。

```text
Hearing the news, the rabbit became excited.
听到这个消息后，兔子变得兴奋。
```

相当于时间状语从句：

```text
When the rabbit heard the news, it became excited.
```

```text
Not knowing what to eat, the rabbit skipped lunch.
不知道吃什么，兔子没吃午饭。
```

相当于原因状语从句：

```text
Because the rabbit didn't know what to eat, it skipped lunch.
```

使用时，分词的逻辑主语通常要与主句主语一致。

```text
❌ Hearing the news, the game became exciting.
```

- `the game` 不能“听到消息”，逻辑主语错误。

```text
✅ Hearing the news, the rabbit became excited.
```

## 过去分词

### 基本形式

过去分词通常表示被动、完成或状态。

```text
eat → eaten
see → seen
grow → grown
tell → told
finish → finished
```

过去分词本身不直接表示过去时间。

```text
The rabbit will eat minced carrots next week.
兔子下周会吃切碎的胡萝卜。
```

- `minced` 是过去分词作定语，表示“被切碎的”，不表示“过去”。

### 过去分词作定语

过去分词通常作后置或前置定语，表示被动或完成。

```text
a baked carrot
一根烤好的胡萝卜

a minced carrot
一根切碎的胡萝卜

a small room
一个小房间

a painted room
一个粉刷过的房间
```

```text
The carrot eaten by the rabbit is delicious.
被兔子吃掉的胡萝卜很美味。
```

### 过去分词作表语

```text
The rabbit is interested in growing carrots.
兔子对种胡萝卜感兴趣。
```

过去分词作表语常表示主语的感受或状态。

```text
The story is interesting.
故事很有趣。

The rabbit is interested.
兔子感兴趣。

The rabbit was frightened.
兔子感到害怕。

The rabbit was frightening.
兔子很吓人。
```

一般规律：

| 形式 | 常见含义 |
| --- | --- |
| `-ing` 形容词 | 令人……的；使人产生某种感受 |
| `-ed` 形容词 | 感到……的；受到某种影响的 |

```text
The movie is boring.
这部电影很无聊。

I am bored.
我感到无聊。
```

### 过去分词作宾语补足语

```text
The rabbit found his carrots stolen.
兔子发现自己的胡萝卜被偷了。
```

```text
find / keep / leave / have / get + 宾语 + 过去分词
```

表示宾语处于被动或完成状态。

```text
I found the door closed.
我发现门关着。
```

### 过去分词作状语

过去分词短语可表示时间、原因、条件、让步、方式或伴随。

```text
Seen from the hill, the carrot field looks beautiful.
从山上看，胡萝卜地看起来很美。
```

```text
Born in a rabbit family, the rabbit has been growing carrots all his life.
兔子出生在一个兔子家庭，此后一直种胡萝卜。
```

```text
Given another chance, the rabbit would go to the wolf's party.
如果再给兔子一次机会，他会参加狼的聚会。
```

过去分词的逻辑主语通常与主句主语一致。

```text
✅ Seen from the hill, the carrot field looks beautiful.
❌ Seeing from the hill, the carrot field looks beautiful.
```

- `carrot field` 是“被看见”，用过去分词 `seen`。
- `carrot field` 不能主动“看”，不能用 `seeing`。

## 分词结构的高级用法

并列句或复合句常可简化为“主句 + 分词短语”的结构，使表达更紧凑。

```text
The work was finished, and the rabbit went home.
```

```text
After the work was finished, the rabbit went home.
```

```text
The work finished, the rabbit went home.
```

```text
The weather permitting, the rabbit will go out.
```

```text
If the weather permits, the rabbit will go out.
```

`The work finished` 和 `The weather permitting` 都属于独立主格结构（详见 [[独立主格 Absolute Construction]]）：它们有自己的逻辑主语，整体作状语。

## 非谓语动词快速判断

| 形式 | 优先判断 | 常见含义 | 关联专题 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `to do` | 不定式 | 目的、将来、具体动作 | [[使役动词 Causative Verb]] |
| `doing` 像名词 | 动名词 | 动作、习惯、用途 | [[名词 noun]] |
| `doing` 像形容词或状语 | 现在分词 | 主动、进行、伴随 | [[形容词 adjective]] · [[副词 adverb]] |
| `done` | 过去分词 | 被动、完成、状态 | [[被动 Passive Voice]] |

## 常见错误

```text
❌ The rabbit enjoys to eat carrots.
✅ The rabbit enjoys eating carrots.
```

```text
❌ The rabbit made the wolf to laugh.
✅ The rabbit made the wolf laugh.
```
- `make` 属于使役动词，接动词原形（参见 [[使役动词 Causative Verb]]）。

```text
❌ I look forward to see you.
✅ I look forward to seeing you.
```
- `look forward to` 中的 `to` 是介词，后面接动名词（参见 [[介词 preposition]]）。

```text
❌ Seeing from the hill, the carrot field looks beautiful.
✅ Seen from the hill, the carrot field looks beautiful.
```
- 分词作状语时，逻辑主语是 `the carrot field`，表被动须用过去分词（参见 [[被动 Passive Voice]]）。

```text
❌ The rabbit has many carrots eating.
✅ The rabbit has many carrots to eat.
```
