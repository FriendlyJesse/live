# Brain drain

Brain drain, which is the action of having highly skilled and educated people leaving their country to work abroad, has become one of developing countries’ concerns. Brain drain is also referred to as human capital flight. More and more third world scientists-and-technology-educated people are heading for more prosperous countries seeking higher wages and better working conditions. This has of course serious consequences on the sending countries.

While many people believe that immigration is a personal choice that must be understood and respected, others look at the phenomenon from a different perspective. What makes those educated people leave their countries should be seriously considered and a distinction between push and pull factors must be made. Push factors include low wages and lack of satisfactory working and living conditions. Social unrest, political conflicts and wars may also be determining causes. The pull factors, however, include freedom for research.

Brain drain has negative impact on the sending countries’ economic prosperity and competitiveness. It reduces the number of dynamic and creative people who can contribute to the development of their country. Likewise, with more entrepreneurs（企业家）taking their investments abroad, developing countries are missing an opportunity of wealth creation. This has also negative consequences on tax revenue and employment.

Most of the measures taken so far have not had any success in softening the effects of brain drain. One option is to provide adequate working and living conditions in the sending countries.

Another option should involve encouraging the expatriates（侨民）to contribute their skill to the development of their countries without necessarily moving back home.

### Main Idea

人才流失是发展中国家面临的一个问题。高技能、高学历人才因本国的 低工资、较差的工作和生活条件，以及社会或政治动荡等推力因素， 流向工资和工作条件更优、科研更自由的富裕国家。人才流失会削弱 输出国的经济发展和竞争力，并影响财富创造、税收和就业。虽然既有措施大多未能有效缓解其影响，但改善本国工作生活条件、鼓励侨民 贡献技能是可能的应对办法。
### Paragraph Idea

1. 人才流失（human capital flight）指高技能、高学历人才离开本国到国外工作； 越来越多发展中国家的科技人才前往富裕国家寻求更高工资和更好工作条件， 这会给输出国带来严重后果。
2. 推力：低工资、缺少令人满意的工作和生活条件、社会动荡、政治冲突与战争。 拉力：科研自由；前文还提到更高工资和更好的工作条件。
3. 人才流失削弱输出国的经济繁荣与竞争力，减少有活力、有创造力的人才； 企业家带走投资，也使发展中国家失去财富创造机会，进而影响税收与就业。
4. 对策一：既有措施大多未成功缓解影响；可以改善输出国的工作和生活条件。
5. 对策二：鼓励侨民即使不回国，也以其技能参与本国发展。

解析：
- `brain drain` ：人才流失
- `human capital flight`: 人力资本外流
- `A is referred to as B`：A 被称为 B
- `heading for`：前往...
- `seeking`：寻求
- `sending`：输出
- `impact`：影响
- `competitiveness`：竞争力
- `the number of`：...的数量
	- `the number of dynamic and creative people`：有活力和创造力的人的数量
- `Likewise`：同样的

## Notes

push：本国条件差，促使人离开
pull：外国条件好，吸引人前往
effects：输出国损失人才、投资、税收与就业
solutions：改善本国条件 + 动员海外侨民贡献

你不是“读不懂”，而是复述时会用常识替换原文词、或不小心把否定句反过来。以后写完 Main Idea，专门检查三类词：**否定词**（not / no）、**程度词**（most / some）、**范围词**（effects / causes / solutions）。
