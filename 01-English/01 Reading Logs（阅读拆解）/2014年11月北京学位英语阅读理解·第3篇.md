# Electronic Waste—a Global Problem

Computer technology is advancing so fast that old hardware quickly becomes completely obsolete. The electronic waste (e-waste) from this constantly growing field is polluting the environment, both here and abroad.

Computers contain toxic (有毒的) materials such as lead. Despite the danger of throwing these hazardous materials in a landfill (垃圾填埋场), that's exactly where tons of computers end up. Americans reuse or recycle only about 10 percent of the 50 million computers they replace each year, according to ABC News.

Eighty percent is being stockpiled (囤积), which could create even bigger problems in the future, and the remaining 10 percent is landfilled. Throwing e-waste in landfills creates a potential for toxic waste leak into our soil and groundwater. Because standards for landfills are tougher in the United States than in many other countries, e-waste is often exported, especially to some developing countries.

Some countries are creating policies to deal with the growing e-waste problem. In the Netherlands, you can bring your old computer to the seller when buying a new one, and the seller must by law accept it free of charge. (15A) Japan passed a law in 2001 requiring producers to recycle certain parts.

In the United States, a movement called the Computer TakeBack Campaign is demanding that producers take more responsibility for disposing of (处理) old computers. California and Massachusetts recently prohibited certain computer parts in landfills, while Apple and IBM take back computers for about a $30 fee.

Gateway is one step ahead: They will pay you $50 for recycling your old computer when you buy a new one from them. Lastly, many nonprofit programs accept used equipment, and services have popped up that distribute old computers to schools and other organizations.

### Main Idea

随着计算机技术快速发展，旧硬件很快被淘汰，电子垃圾正在污染国内外环境。电脑含有铅等有毒物质，但美国每年淘汰的约 5,000 万台电脑中，仅约 10% 被再利用或回收；约 80% 被囤积，约 10% 被填埋。填埋可能污染土壤和地下水，而由于一些其他国家的填埋标准不如美国严格，电子垃圾还常被出口到这些国家，尤其是发展中国家。

为应对电子垃圾问题，荷兰、日本等国家通过规定回收责任来处理旧电脑；美国的环保组织和部分州也推动生产者承担更多责任。企业则以付费回收、补贴回收等方式参与，非营利机构也会接收二手设备并将其分配给学校和其他组织。

### Paragraph Idea

1. **问题引入**：电脑技术更新很快，旧硬件迅速淘汰；电子垃圾污染国内外环境。
2. **有毒性与低回收率**：电脑含有铅等有毒材料，但美国每年替换的 5,000 万台电脑中，仅约 10% 被再利用或回收。
3. **处置后果和出口**：大量旧电脑被囤积或填埋，可能污染土壤和地下水；电子垃圾也常出口到填埋标准相对不严格的国家。
4. **国家政策**：荷兰要求卖家免费接收消费者在购买新电脑时交回的旧电脑；日本要求生产商回收某些部件。
5. **美国组织、州与企业措施**：Computer TakeBack Campaign 要求生产商对旧电脑处置承担更多责任；加州和马萨诸塞州限制部分电脑部件填埋；Apple 和 IBM 提供约 30 美元的有偿回收。
6. **Gateway 与非营利组织**：Gateway 在用户购买新机时，支付 50 美元鼓励其回收旧机；非营利项目接收二手设备，并提供给学校和其他组织。
