# The Harmer Family And Their House

Edward and Robert Harmer were both born in the house on Cranberry Lane. The map of their town had a special note about the Harmer house and its historical importance. Their great-grandfather had built it with his own two hands in 1850. It was the house their grandfather and father had been born in. It was the house that had all their memories.

The boys had always been close as children. They were only two years apart, and although they sometimes fought as brothers do, mostly they were inseparable.

There was never a dull moment in the Harmer household. The energetic brothers spent their summers running around. They would go fishing in the local river, and try to catch the biggest fish in the world. The whole area was full of secret hiding places where they could go and no one would find them. And they really needed hiding places. The boys were constantly getting into trouble, much to the worry of their parents. Whether it was accidentally throwing footballs through the neighbors’ windows, pouring paint onto Mrs. Pinkiewitz’s rose bushes or putting shaving cream all over Uncle Bart’s car, the boys somehow always got away from punishments.

When they got older, Edward and Robert spent less time around the house and more time with their friends. But their house was still the best place to have a party. With its huge lawn, it was perfect for picnics. Their mother made the best fried chicken in town, and everybody knew it. Holidays were also a popular time at the Harmers’ house. Every New Year’s Eve they threw a big party. Practically the whole town came. Their mother would start planning the party on Thanksgiving. It was the biggest event in their town. And when the boys went off to college, they always brought friends home to share the holiday spirit.

### Main Idea

本文讲述 Harmer 两兄弟与家族老宅的故事。老宅由曾祖父建于1850年， 承载了家族几代人的出生与成长记忆；兄弟俩在这里度过亲密而淘气的童年， 长大后这座房子仍是家人和全镇居民聚会、庆祝节日的重要场所。

### Paragraph Idea

1. Edward 和 Robert 出生在 Cranberry Lane 的老宅。房子由曾祖父于1850年亲手建造， 祖父和父亲也出生于此；它具有历史意义，并承载着一家人的记忆。
2. 两兄弟相差两岁。虽然偶尔像普通兄弟一样争吵，但大多数时候形影不离。
3. 兄弟俩精力旺盛，夏天常在外玩耍、钓鱼和探索秘密藏身处。 他们经常惹麻烦，因此需要躲藏；但总能设法逃过惩罚。
4. 兄弟长大后更多和朋友相处，但老宅仍是举办野餐、节日聚会的好地方。 母亲以镇上最好的炸鸡闻名；每年除夕全镇几乎都会来参加大派对， 母亲从感恩节就开始筹备。兄弟上大学后，回家过节时也常带朋友来分享节日气氛。
