# Get Rid of the Penny or Not?

Jarden Zinc（锌）Products, a large zinc plant a few miles outside Greenville, Tennessee, has a special claim. Since 1982, it has been the only supplier of pennies blanks for the U.S. Mint（铸币厂）. It’s a good business for Jarden—since 2000, the company has earned more than $800 million. But it may not be a good deal for the U.S.

The value of the penny has been dropping for years. In 2006, it began to cost more than a penny to make a penny. It now costs 2 cents to produce a 1¢ coin. Many countries have stopped using pennies. Is it time for the U.S. to do the same?

Jarden and the zinc industry are fighting to keep the penny. Since 2006, Jarden has given $1.2 million to Americans for Common Cents（ACC）. The group’s mission is to keep the penny in use. Mark Weller is ACC’s executive director. He argues that there are three main reasons for keeping the penny: Without it, we would become more reliant on the five-cent coin, which also has problems; charities（慈善机构）that depend on driveways would not be able to raise as much money; and a 2012 survey shows that 67% of Americans want to keep the penny. Many people surveyed said they feared there would end up paying more for products.

Many experts disagree with ACC. They point to the dozens of countries that have gotten rid of their lowest-value coins without raising prices for consumers. And charities don’t seem too concerned either.

President Barack Obama says the mint could explore using cheaper metals to make pennies. Steel is less expensive than zinc. Pennies get 97.5% zinc and 2.5% copper. But no matter what it is made of, the penny’s days may be numbered. Most in-store purchases are now made with credit cards, not cash. Is it time for a change?

5. 奥巴马总统认为，美国铸币厂可以研究用成本更低的金属制造一美分硬币，例如钢比锌便宜。可是，无论一美分硬币由什么材料制成，它可能都时日无多：如今大多数店内消费已通过信用卡而非现金完成。

## 📘 Sentence Breakdown

### 🌟 Sentence 1

>They point to the dozens of countries 
>	that have gotten rid of their lowest-value coins 
>		without raising prices for consumers.

许多国家废除最低面额货币后没有造成物价上涨

解析：
- **point to**：指出、列举、援引……作为证据或论据；
- **without raising prices for consumers**：伴随结果/情况，修饰 _have gotten rid of_，即“取消时并没有使消费者支付的价格上涨”。

类似例句：
- They solved the problem **without asking for help**.  
    他们没有寻求帮助，就解决了问题。
- She finished the report **without making any mistakes**.  
    她完成了报告，没有出任何错误。
- The company reduced costs **without cutting jobs**.  
    该公司降低了成本，同时没有裁员。

### 🌟 Sentence 2

> He argues that there are three main reasons for keeping the penny: Without it, we would become more reliant on the five-cent coin, which also has problems; charities（慈善机构）that depend on driveways would not be able to raise as much money; and a 2012 survey shows that 67% of Americans want to keep the penny. Many people surveyed said they feared there would end up paying more for products.

他辩称保留一美分硬币有三个主要原因：

解析：
- **Without it**：如果没有它；这里 _it_ 指 penny。
- **we would become**：我们将会变得……
- **more reliant on**：更依赖……
- **which also has problems**：它也存在问题；_which_ 指 five-cent coin。
	- 这里是非限定性定语从句：修饰物时使用 **which**，不能用 **that**
- **that depend on ...**：定语从句，修饰 charities，“依赖……的慈善机构”。
- **would not be able to raise**：将无法筹集。
- **as much money**：那么多钱／同样多的钱。
- **survey**：调查
- **Many people surveyed**：许多接受调查的人。
- **would end up paying more**：最终会支付更多钱。
	- **end up paying more**：最终做了某事／结果做了某事
- **for products**：购买商品时。
