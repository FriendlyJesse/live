# Ready for a One-way Trip to Mars

Humans may not have landed on Mars (火星) just yet, but that isn’t stopping a European company from devising a plan to send four people to the Red Planet within the next few years. This project, called Mars One, aims to send a small group of people to Mars in 2022 and eventually establish a permanent colony on the planet.

“Everything we need to go to Mars exists,” said Mars One co-founder Bas Lansdorp in March 2014. “We have the rockets to send people to Mars, the equipment to land on Mars, the robots to prepare the settlement for humans. For a one-way mission, all the technology exists.” Yet the four astronauts (宇航员) chosen for the trip will be stuck on Mars — forever.

And despite Mars One’s thorough planning, there are a number of challenges that may prevent the mission from ever taking place. The biggest roadblock could be the mission’s huge cost ($6 billion). However, Lansdorp is confident that Mars One will be able to fund the project by selling the broadcast rights for the mission and subsequent experiences living on the planet.

Those broadcast rights will also play a part in helping to select the people who will be sent to Mars. Lansdorp said the company will hold a selection process similar to a reality show. Lansdorp is expecting at least 1 million applications from people around the world. In addition to the cost, several other potential problems could inhibit (阻止) the mission to Mars.

“It’s even more challenging to send people there with life support, with food, with air, with all the other things like books, entertainment, means of communication and means of providing for their own resources for a long stay on Mars,” said Adam Baker, a senior lecturer in space engineering at Kingston University in London. “The sheer size of the rockets you need to do this would be absolutely colossal.”

## 📘 Sentence Breakdown

### 🌟 Sentence 1

> This project, called Mars One, aims to send a small group of people to Mars in 2022 and eventually establish a permanent colony on the planet.

这个名为“火星一号”的项目旨在于 2022 年将一小组人送往火星，并最终在火星上建立永久殖民地。。

拆解：
- **called Mars One**：叫作 Mars One 的
	- 过去分词短语作后置定语
	- 完整写法：_This project, which is called Mars One, ..._
- **aims to do**：旨在做……
- **send a small group of people to Mars**：把一小群人送往火星

### ### 🌟 Sentence 2

>We have the rockets to send people to Mars, the equipment to land on Mars, the robots to prepare the settlement for humans.

我们拥有把人送往火星的火箭、用于在火星着陆的设备，以及为人类准备定居点的机器人。

拆解：
- the rockets to send people to Mars
  用来把人送往火星的火箭
- the equipment to land on Mars
  用于在火星着陆的设备
- the robots to prepare the settlement for humans
  用来为人类准备定居点的机器人
- prepare A for B
  为 B 准备 A

关键理解：
>prepare the settlement for humans
>为人类准备定居点。

##### 记录点

> **the + 名词 + to do**  
> 用来做……的某物

例如：

> the money to buy a computer  
> 买电脑的钱

### 🌟 Sentence 3

>Yet the four astronauts chosen for the trip **will be stuck** on Mars — forever.

然而，被选中参加这次旅行的宇航员永远的被困在火星。

拆解：
- Yet: 然而
	- 表示转折；前面 “技术已经具备”，这里补充 “但他们回不来“。
- chosen for the trip: 被选中参加这次的旅行
	- 过去分词短语作定语
	- 完整写法：_the four astronauts who are chosen for the trip_
- **will be stuck on Mars**：将被困在／滞留在火星

#### 记录点

> **noun + done + 其他成分**  
> 被……的某人／某物

> **be stuck in/on …**  
> 困在……；卡在……

### 🌟 sentence 4

> And despite Mars One’s thorough planning, there are a number of challenges that may prevent the mission from ever taking place.

尽管火星一号有周密的计划，仍有许多调整可能会使这项任务根本无法实现。

拆解：
- **despite + 名词**：尽管……
- **a number of + 可数名词复数**：许多……
- **challenges that may prevent ...**：可能阻止……的挑战
	- `that` 引导定语从句，修饰 **challenges**
- prevent **the mission from ever taking place**：阻止该任务最终发生／实现
	- **prevent A from doing**：阻止 A 做某事
	- **take place**：发生、举行、进行
	- **ever**：在这里表示“在任何时候、究竟、最终”，放在否定或带有否定含义的语境中加强语气
	- **ever taking place**：最终发生

### 🌟 sentence 5

> It’s even more challenging to send people there with life support, with food, with air, with all the other things like books, entertainment, means of communication and means of providing for their own resources for a long stay on Mars.

要把人送往火星，同时还要带上生命维持系统、食物、空气，以及书籍、娱乐、通信手段等各种长期居住所需物品，会更加困难。

拆解：
- **It is + adj. + to do**：做某事是……的
- **even more challenging**：更加具有挑战性／更困难
- **send people there**：把人送到那里
	- `there` 指 Mars（火星）
- **with life support**：带着生命维持系统
- **with food / with air**：带着食物／空气
- **all the other things like ...**：以及其他各种东西，例如……
	- books：书籍
	- entertainment：娱乐消遣
	- means of providing for their own resources for a long stay on Mars：保障他们长期在火星生活所需资源的手段
- **means of communication**：通信手段
- **means of providing for their own resources**：保障／提供他们自身所需资源的手段
	- means of providing ...：提供／保障……的手段
	- providing for their own resources：他们自身所需资源
- **for a long stay on Mars**：为了在火星长期停留

#### 记录点

> **means of doing sth.**  
> 做某事的手段

> **provide for sb.**  
> 为某人提供生活所需；保障某人的生活

例如：

> The family needs a means of providing for itself.  
> 这个家庭需要一种保障自身生活所需的方式。
