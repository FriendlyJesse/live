# An Oasis on Wheels

In 2011, Josh Trautwein was working as a nutrition educator in Boston. He learned that the neighborhood's only grocery store was closing for a year of repair. The families were having a tough time finding another nearby market that sold fresh produce.

Residents of Trautwein's Boston community are not alone when it comes to lacking supermarkets that sell fresh and healthy food. According to the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA), 17% of Americans lived more than half a mile from a supermarket in 2017. The USDA uses the term “food deserts” to describe neighborhoods that lack access to nutritious food.

Trautwein began to imagine a new kind of market, an oasis (绿洲) for communities in need. In 2013, he teamed up with Annika Morgan to launch Fresh Truck, a school bus that functions as a grocery store on wheels. Now, with two mobile markets on the road, Fresh Truck provides communities in the Boston area with year-round access to fresh produce such as apples, carrots and eggplants at 17 locations.

But Morgan says the term “food deserts” doesn't paint a full picture. “The idea of food deserts implies that there is no food around” at all, she says, “when in reality there are a lot of barriers that prevent access.” These barriers include the high cost of fresh produce and people's lack of familiarity with the produce that is available.

Getting state and local officials enthusiastic about food issues is one way to remove these barriers, says Anne Palmer. Her group campaigns for the need for policies that connect people with healthy food. “It's been fun to watch communities use food as a tool to organize,” Palmer says. “The best thing about an issue like this is that you can do something about it.

    - A. educate people on available fresh produce

    - C. provide healthy food at affordable prices

15. Which of the following can be the best title for the passage? ==A==
    - A. An Oasis on Wheels
    - B. A Nutrition War
    - C. Food as a Tool
    - D. Food Deserts or Not

解析：
- `doesn't paint a full picture`：不能完整反应事实
- `term`：术语
- **campaign for**：为……而呼吁、倡导
- `policies`：政策
- **connect people with healthy food**：让人们获得健康食品
- `fresh produce`：新鲜农产品、果蔬
- **lack access to**：缺乏获得……的渠道/机会
- **food desert**：食品荒漠区
- **on wheels**：流动的；车载的
- **barrier to access**：获取方面的障碍
- **year-round access**：全年都能获得的机会
- `close for + 时间`：关闭一年 / 停业一年
- `for repair`：为了维修；因维修而……
- `tough`：坚固的
- `familiarity`：熟悉

## 📘 Parse

### Main Idea

波士顿营养教育工作者 Trautwein 发现，许多社区的居民难以方便地获得新鲜、健康的食品。为此，他与 Morgan 将校车改造成流动杂货店 Fresh Truck，把新鲜农产品带到社区。文章同时指出，所谓“食品荒漠”并不只是附近没有食物，还包括价格高、居民不熟悉农产品等获取障碍；因此，社区组织呼吁州和地方政府通过相关政策，让更多人能够获得健康食品。

### Paragraph Idea

1. 2011 年，营养教育工作者 Trautwein 在波士顿工作时得知，所在街区唯一的杂货店将因维修关闭一年。当地家庭很难在附近找到出售新鲜农产品的其他市场。
2. Trautwein 所在社区并非唯一面临健康食品获取困难的地区。美国农业部称，2017 年有 17% 的美国人居住地距离超市超过半英里，并用 **food deserts** 一词描述那些难以获得营养食品的社区。
3. Trautwein 想建立一种服务有需要社区的新型市场，即“社区绿洲”。2013 年，他与 Morgan 创办 Fresh Truck，把校车改造成流动杂货店；如今两辆流动市场在波士顿地区的 17 个地点全年供应苹果、胡萝卜、茄子等新鲜农产品。
4. Morgan 认为，“食品荒漠”这个术语不能完整反映现实，因为它容易使人认为当地完全没有食物。实际上，健康食品的高价格，以及居民对可获得农产品不熟悉等因素，都会阻碍人们获得健康食品。
5. Palmer 认为，让州和地方官员重视食品问题，是消除这些障碍的一种办法。她所在的组织倡导制定相关政策，把人们与健康食品连接起来；她也强调，社区可以通过食品议题组织行动并推动改变。

## 📘 Sentence Breakdown

### 🌟 Sentence 1

>The families were having a tough time finding another nearby market that sold fresh produce.

当地家庭很难找到另一家出售新鲜农产品的附近市场。

🌟 Sentence 2

>when in reality there are a lot of barriers that prevent access.

但实际上，存在许多阻碍人们获得健康食品的因素。

🌟 Sentence 3

>Getting state and local officials enthusiastic about food issues is one way to remove these barriers, says Anne Palmer.

Anne Palmer 说，让州和地方官员重视并积极支持食品问题，是消除这些障碍的一种方式。

tips：
- Getting A enthusiastic about B is one way to do C.：让 A 对 B 产生热情/积极关注，是做 C 的一种方式。

🌟 Sentence 4

>Her group campaigns for the need for policies that connect people with healthy food.

她所在的组织倡导制定相关政策，使人们能够获得健康食品。

tips：
- `campaign for sth.`：倡导、呼吁支持某事。
- **the need for policies**：需要制定政策。
- **connect people with healthy food**：让人们与健康食品建立可获得的联系，即“使人们能够获得健康食品”。

🌟 Sentence 5

>“It's been fun to watch communities use food as a tool to organize,” Palmer says.

Palmer 说：“看到社区把食品议题作为组织和团结居民的工具，是一件很有意思的事。”

🌟 Sentence 6

>The best thing about an issue like this is that you can do something about it.

像这样的问题，最好的一点在于人们可以采取行动来改善或解决它。

- **do something about sth.**：采取行动处理/改善/解决某事。
- 不能直译成“你做点什么”，那样不完整，也没有表达出“针对这个问题行动”的意思。
