# Article 

## 📘 Article Summary（一句话）

The passage argues that starting pre-school earlier can reduce inequality between rich and poor children and benefit society in the long term.
## 📘 Sentence Breakdown  

### 🌟 Sentance 1（原句）

The first year of school in America, known as kindergarten, usually begins between the ages of five and six.
#### Structure（结构）

主干：
- The first year of school in American ... **begins**

修饰：
- of school（修饰 year）
- in America（修饰 school / 整体背景）
- known as kindergarten（过去分词作后置定语，修饰 _the first year_）

时间状语：
- between the ages of five and six
#### Key Points（关键点）

1. known as kindergarten 
	- 过去分词短语 → 修饰 the first year
	- 相当于：which is **known** as kindergarten
2. between ... and ...：
	- 固定时间表达（在……之间）  
	- 作 **begins 的时间状语**

#### Chinese（中文理解）

在美国，学校的第一年（称为幼儿园），通常于 5 到 6 岁之间

#### Rewrite（改写）

In America, the first year school, known as kindergarten, usually begins between the ages of five and six.

### 🌟 Sentance 2（原句）

Among rich countries such a late start is very strange.
#### Structure（结构）

主干：
- such a late start is very strange

范围状语：
- Amon rich countries
#### Key Points（关键点）

1. among  → 在...之中（强调“群体里”，多于三个）
2. such a + 名词 → 这样一种
3. late start → 开始得很晚

#### Chinese（中文理解）

在发达国家里，这种晚入学现象很不寻常。

#### Rewrite（改写）

In rich countries, such a late start is very strange.

### 🌟 Sentance 3（原句）

This statement has some support, as it is clear from research into vocabulary that youngsters from poor families enter kindergarten well behind those from rich families — a disadvantage that usually lasts a lifetime.
#### Structure（结构）

This statement has some support, as it is clear from research into vocabulary

主干：
- This statement has some support -> 这个说法有一些依据
- it is clear from search into vocabulary that ... → 很明显

**that 从句：**
youngsters from poor families enter kindergarten well behind those from rich families

主干：
- youngsters ... enter kindergarten behind those

修饰：
- from poor families（修饰 youngsters）
- those from rich families （those = youngsters）

**同位语补充说明：**
— a disadvantage that usually lasts a lifetime.

拆解：
- a disadvantage → 这种落后是一种劣势
- that usually last a lifetime（定语从句） → 这种劣势通常会持续一生

#### Key Points（关键点）

1. research into vocabulary  → 对词汇的研究
2. well behind → 明显落后
3. those → youngsters （代替前面名词，避免重复）
4. 破折号（—）→ 补充说明 / 解释前面整句话

#### Chinese（中文理解）

这一说法有一定依据，因为词汇研究清楚的表面，来自贫困家庭的还在进入幼儿园时明显落后于富裕家庭的孩子——而这种劣势通常会持续一生

#### Rewrite（改写）

This statement is supported by research showing that children from poor families start kindergarten behind those from rich families.

### 🌟 Sentance 4（原句）

Children from households on welfare knew 525 words by the age of three, while the children of professionals had mastered 1, 116.
#### Structure（结构）

主干：
- Children knew 525 words -> 孩子们理解 525 个词汇
- children had mastered 1,116 -> 另一些孩子会 1116 个词汇

修饰：
1. Children from households on welfare → 来自领取“福利家庭”的孩子
2. welfare → 社会福利
3. the children of professionals → 专业认识的孩子（比如医生、律师、白领）

时间状语：
1. by the age of three → 到三岁的时候

#### Key Points（关键点）

1. while → 表对比 “而 / 然而”
2. households on welfare → 取社会福利的家庭（低收入）
3. children of professionals → 专业人士家庭的孩子（高收入）
4. had mastered → 表示“已经掌握”（比前面更早/更强）

#### Chinese（中文理解）

来自福利家庭的孩子在三岁是掌握了 525 个词，而专业人士家庭的孩子已经掌握了 1116 个词。

#### Rewrite（改写）

By the age of three, children from welfare families knew 525 words, while children of professionals knew 1,116.
