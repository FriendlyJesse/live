# Charles Dickens

Charles Dickens was born in 1812 in Portsmouth, England. He was the second of eight children. His father always had problems with money. Charles was 12 years old, his father went to prison because he was in debt. Charles had to leave school to help his family. He got a job in a dirty, old factory. Charles Dickens never forgot his difficult childhood. Many of his stories and books were about poor people and their problems.

Later, Charles went back to school for two more years. He left school when he was 15 years old to become a newspaper reporter. In 1836, he began to write _The Pickwick Papers_. It was published as a series and was a huge success. By age 24, Dickens was a prominent writer in both Great Britain and the United States.

Many people bought his books, but they also paid to hear him read his stories aloud. Because there was no radio or television, people liked to hear famous writers read in public. Dickens read his works like he was acting in a play. He went on very successful reading tours and earned a lot of money.

Dickens was meticulous (过分注意琐事的). Everything had to be just right. When he worked at home, everything had to be in its place. He worked at a desk by a window that always had a vase (花瓶) of flowers and the same ornaments (装饰物) on it.

He wrote 2,000 words a day and he required complete quiet while he wrote. He divided his page into three parts, and each side had notes in difficult colors. The main writing was in middle, the story notes were in the right margin, and the chapter notes were in the left margin. He also cared a lot about his appearance.

### Paragraph Idea

1. 狄更斯于1812年出生在英国朴次茅斯，在家中八个孩子里排行第二。父亲长期有经济困难，后来因欠债入狱。12岁的狄更斯不得不辍学，到一家又脏又旧的工厂工作来帮助家庭。这段艰难的童年经历使他后来许多故事和书籍都关注穷人及其困境。
2. 后来，狄更斯回到学校又读了两年书，15岁时离校成为一名**报纸记者**。1836年，他开始创作《匹克威克外传》（_The Pickwick Papers_），该作品以连载形式出版并获得巨大成功。到24岁时，他已经成为英国和美国都很有名的作家。
3. 许多人购买狄更斯的书；由于当时没有**收音机**或电视，人们也愿意付费听著名作家在公众场合朗读作品。狄更斯朗读自己的作品时像在演戏一样生动。他通过非常成功的**巡回公开朗读活动**赚了很多钱。
4. 狄更斯做事极其细致，对环境要求严格，希望每样东西都摆放在正确的位置。他在家工作时，桌子靠着窗户，窗边总放着一只插花的花瓶和相同的装饰物。
5. 狄更斯每天写2,000个单词，并要求写作时绝对安静。他把每页纸分为三个部分，并用不同颜色做笔记：正文写在中间，故事笔记写在右边空白处，章节笔记写在左边空白处。他也非常在意自己的**外表和仪表**。

## 📘 Sentence Breakdown

### 🌟 Sentence 1

> He divided his page into three parts,  
> 	and each side had notes in different colors:  
> 		the main writing was in middle,  
> 		the story notes were in the right margin,  
> 		and the chapter notes were in the left margin.

#### Translate（译文）

> 他把每页纸分成三个部分，每一边都用不同颜色的笔做笔记：主要内容写在中间，故事笔记写在右边空白处，章节笔记写在左边空白处。

#### Key Points（要点）

- **divide ... into ...**：把……划分为……（常用[[介词 preposition]]搭配）；
- **and**：[[连词 conjunction|并列连词]]连接两个独立主句；
- **冒号作用**：[[标点符号 punctuation|冒号]]引出后方的并列详细说明分述；
- **the main writing ... the story notes ... and the chapter notes ...**：三个平行排比句结构，谓语动词均为[[系动词 linking verbs|系动词]] _was/were_ + [[介词 preposition]]短语作表语。
