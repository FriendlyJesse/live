# The Oldest Message in a Bottle

In 1866, sailors on a German ship called Paula threw a bottle with a message into waters hundreds of miles off the western coast of Australia.

It’s believed to be the oldest-known message in a bottle, in terms of the amount of time that has passed between when it was written and when it was found. Before this discovery, Guinness (吉尼斯) World Records said that the oldest was 108 years old, found in Germany in 2015. Australian and German researchers worked together to check whether the note is real or not.

Tonya Illman was walking on the beach in January when she spotted the old bottle. “I picked it up thinking it might look nice on display in my home,” Illman says, according to an account on her husband’s website. The bottle was partially filled with sand. Shortly after, Illman’s son’s girlfriend poured out the contents and found a tightly rolled note covered in a piece of string.

Too damp to open initially, according to the account, the group waited for it to dry and were amazed to see that it was carefully handwritten in German. “The first thing that caught my eye was the year 1866,” said Illman’s husband Kym. “It seemed totally unlikely to us that the note and bottle could have lasted that long.”

The pair reached out to the Western Australian Museum, which set out to study the document.

“Extraordinary finds need extraordinary evidence to support them, so we contacted colleagues in Germany for help to find more information,” Ross Anderson, who is in charge of works of art in the museum, said in a statement. But this message is no SOS or love letter. According to Guinness World Records, the second oldest message was found.

## 📘 Parse

### Main Idea

1866 年，水手们在澳大利亚西海岸数百英里外扔了一个漂流瓶。Illman 在海边散步时发现了这个瓶子，根据澳大利亚和德国的专业人士的研究，这不是求救信或情书。最后吉尼斯记录认为它是第二古老的信息。
### Paragraph Idea

1. 1886 年，德国帆船 Paula 号上的水手在距离澳大利亚西海岸数百英里的海域投下一个装有纸条的漂流瓶。
2. 这张纸条被认为是已知最古老的漂流瓶信息。在它被发现之前，吉尼斯世界纪录中的最老纪录是一张在德国于 2015 年发现、已有 108 年历史的纸条。澳大利亚和德国研究人员共同核验新发现是否真实。
3. 一月，Tonya Illman 在海滩散步时发现了这个瓶子。瓶中部分装着沙子。之后，Illman 儿子的女朋友倒出瓶内物品，发现了一张用细绳紧紧卷起的纸条。
4. 纸条起初太潮湿，无法打开。他们等纸条变干后，惊讶地发现它是用德语认真手写的。Kym 看到纸条上的年份后十分惊讶，难以相信瓶子和纸条能够保存如此之久。
5. Tonya 和 Kym 联系了西澳大利亚博物馆，博物馆随即开始研究这份文件。
6. 博物馆工作人员 Ross Anderson 表示，为获得更多证据，博物馆方面联系了德国同行。这张纸条既不是求救信，也不是情书。旧的记录被更新为第二古老的信息。
