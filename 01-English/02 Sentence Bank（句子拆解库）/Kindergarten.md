## Sentence
The man who lives next door is a doctor.

## 1. Main Structure
The man is a doctor

## 2. Clause Breakdown
who lives next door → 定语从句

## 3. Grammar Type
- 定语从句
- 主系表结构

## 4. Meaning
住在隔壁的那个人是医生

## 5. Rewrite (必须)
The man is a doctor. He lives next door.

## 6. Pattern
S + who + V + ... + be + N
